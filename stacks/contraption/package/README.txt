Contraption: physics puzzles, for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
===========================================================

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere and double-click stacks_contraption.exe.

Get the ball into the bucket. Pick a piece from the tray in the Contraption window,
then left click to place it (the wheel, or Q and E, turns it; right click takes it
back). Space runs the machine; R resets it; N and P change level. Tick "intended" to
see the designer's solution as ghosts.

Edit data\default.dta in Notepad while the game runs and save: a level changes at
once (move a piece, change a kind's mass or bounce).

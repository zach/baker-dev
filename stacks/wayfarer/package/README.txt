Wayfarer: exploration missions, for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
=============================================================

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere and double-click stacks_wayfarer.exe.

Explore and finish the missions. WASD moves; Q and E (or the mouse with the right
button held) turn the camera; space jumps, and held in the air glides once you have
the glider. Push into a brown face to climb it once you have the gloves. Gold posts are
where your missions point; the Wayfarer window has the map.

Edit data\default.dta in Notepad while the game runs and save: a changed cell is rebuilt
where it stands (move a block, make a wall climbable).

Duel: player versus computer fighting, for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
=====================================================================

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere and double-click stacks_duel.exe.
You are blue, on the left; the CPU is red, on the right.

Keys          A and D (or the arrows) walk; S crouches; hold back to block
              (crouch and hold back to block a low kick).
              J light, K heavy, L kick, I special (crouch + L: low kick).
Gamepad       stick or pad; X light, Y heavy, A kick, B special.
P             pauses; period (.) steps one frame while paused.
F1            shows and hides the panels. The Duel panel sets who plays each
              side (you, a dummy, a CPU level, or a bot), shows every move's
              frame data, and searches for combos.

Edit data\default.dta in Notepad while the game runs and save: the fight uses
the new numbers at once (try "(hit_pause 6" to "(hit_pause 14", or a move's
"(damage" or "(startup"). A mistake is reported in the console window with its
line, and the game keeps its last good data.

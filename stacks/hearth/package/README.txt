Hearth: characters with routines, for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
===============================================================

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere and double-click stacks_hearth.exe.

Watch a household live. The Hearth window picks the household and the speed; pick a
villager to see its needs, a day's timeline of them, and why it chose what it is doing.
Space pauses; 1 to 4 set the speed; tab picks the next villager; N spawns the next
household.

Edit data\default.dta in Notepad while the game runs and save: the household starts
again with the change (a need's decay, a thing's hours, a resident's weights).

Ledger: a tycoon economy, for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
=======================================================

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere and double-click stacks_ledger.exe.

Build farms and ranches, sell what they make, and climb to mills, weavers,
bakeries, and tailors as your earnings unlock them. Prices fall as you flood a
market and recover over the following days. The Ledger window:

  Build / Sell    buy a building, or sell one back at half its cost
  sell            untick a good to keep it in stock instead of selling it
  1x 4x 16x       the clock; a day is five seconds at 1x; "+30 days" jumps ahead
  milestones      the designer's target days, ticked when you reach them

F1 shows and hides Moira's panels. The goods and buildings come from a
spreadsheet (data\economy.dta is its export); the rules and milestones are in
data\default.dta, which you can edit in Notepad while the game runs.

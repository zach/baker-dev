Stuntline: stunt races, for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
=====================================================

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere and double-click stacks_stuntline.exe.
A gamepad is best.

Pad      right trigger: throttle; left trigger: brake (reverse when stopped);
         left stick: steer; A: handbrake.
Keys     W/S or up/down: throttle and brake; A/D or left/right: steer; space: handbrake.
R        puts the car back on the road.   Tab: let the pro AI drive.   N: next track.
F1       shows the panels; the Stuntline window runs every AI driver over the track.

Edit data\default.dta in Notepad while the game runs and save: the track is laid
again from the kit at once (try a piece's length or rise, or the car's max_torque).
